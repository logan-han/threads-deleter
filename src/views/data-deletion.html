deleting your data

Three ways to remove everything Threads Deleter holds about you. All of them take effect immediately.

From your status page

Open the status link you were given when you connected and choose "Erase my token". The stored token and job are deleted straight away and nothing further runs.

By removing the app in Threads

In Threads, go to Settings, then Website permissions, then Apps and websites, and remove Threads Deleter. Threads notifies this service and the stored record is deleted automatically.

By asking

Email logan@han.life with your Threads username and the record will be deleted by hand. Expect a reply within a few days.

What is removed

Everything: the access token, your Threads user ID and username, the job configuration and its counters. Posts that were already deleted from Threads stay deleted, which cannot be reversed.

Records also expire on their own 120 days after they are created.
